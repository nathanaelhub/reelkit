# Terminal

A green phosphor CRT: everything is text or vector line work, typed or traced, glowing a little, with scanlines. Good for tech, hacking, internet culture and anything that should feel like a machine is talking.

- **Text first.** Headlines get a `> ` prompt automatically. Use `data-anim="type"` for headlines and lists; let them type at reading speed.
- **Vector lines only.** Drawings are outlines (`line`, `fill-b`) like an oscilloscope or a vector arcade game. Amber (`fill-a`, `.accent`, `line alt`) marks the important thing.
- **Numbers** read well here: `count` in `display` size.
- **Captions** are upper case with the spoken word in reverse video.
- **Motion:** type, draw, fade, cut. No springs or bounces; things appear the way a screen redraws. Transitions `cut` or `flash`, or `gl:GlitchMemories` for one big moment.
- **Music:** `bed({ mood: 'tense' })`, or `'lofi'`.
